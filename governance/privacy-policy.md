# Privacy Policy — Indonesia Open Justice Observatory (IOJO)

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Legal Alignment:** Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP) & Supreme Court Decree KMA No. 2-144/KMA/SK/VIII/2022 (Public Information Disclosure in Courts).

## 1. Strict PII Exclusion
IOJO curates institutional, statistical, and metadata records. IOJO datasets **strictly exclude**:
- Personal identifiers of individual judges, court clerks, or staff (names, NIP, personal contact details).
- Personal identifiers of litigants, witnesses, victims, or minors (names, NIK, addresses, phone numbers).
- Any sensitive personal data protected under UU PDP or court anonymization rules.

## 2. No Individual Judge Ranking
IOJO prohibits ranking individual judges or attributing case-level performance metrics to named judicial officers.

## 3. Pre-Publication Privacy Audit
Before any dataset is approved for public release, the Governance Agent and human reviewer must verify zero PII leakage across all text and metadata fields.
