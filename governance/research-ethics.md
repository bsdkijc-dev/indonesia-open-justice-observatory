# Research Ethics — Indonesia Open Justice Observatory (IOJO)

1. **Do Not Assume Missing Data = Poor Performance:** Publication gaps may reflect technical, infrastructural, or legal anonymization constraints rather than judicial performance.
2. **Contextualized Comparisons:** Courts must never be compared without accounting for caseload volume, jurisdiction, case mix, reporting period, and data completeness.
3. **No Unvalidated Composite Rankings:** IOJO does not publish composite national judicial rankings without a peer-reviewed, validated methodology and explicit safeguards.
4. **Distinguish Evidence from Policy Opinion:** Empirical findings must be strictly separated from policy interpretations and recommendations.
5. **Reproducibility:** Every published indicator must be accompanied by its exact numerator, denominator, formula, and code.
