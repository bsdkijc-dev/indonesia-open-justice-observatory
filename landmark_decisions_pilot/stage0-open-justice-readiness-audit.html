Nine Existing Repositories Provide Schemas, Court IDs, and Validation Gates

Before creating new data pipelines, Stage 0 audited active repositories under GitHub (bsdkijc-dev) and Kaggle (judicialcollege). Rather than starting from zero, the Open Justice ecosystem already has verified court registries, annual report extractors, regulatory impact records, and fail-closed validation scripts.

Core Open Justice & Court Registry

indonesia-open-justice-observatory (IOJO v1.0) holds 290 DS02 rows and 132 DS06 indicators from 2024–2025 Kepaniteraan annual reports. indonesia-master-court-registry-2026 (v0.8) maps 891 courts, 812 hierarchy edges, and 358 DIPA satker codes.

Transparency Scorecard & JNPDI

indonesian-court-transparency-scorecard provides a CC-BY-4.0 21-column dataset with CI schema checks and zero-imputation pytest suites. indonesia-judiciary-national-prosperity (JNPDI v1.0.0) adds a 12-point validator, fail-closed governance gate, and GitHub Pages portal.

Thematic Observatories & Local AI

IJRIO (v1.19.0) tracks 107 PERMA and 17 Ex-Post RIA clusters. JPAIS runs local Qdrant, Neo4j, and Ollama (qwen2.5:3b + bge-m3) bound to 127.0.0.1, alongside specialized JRC, ILJO, and UMKM observatories.

Public Sources Are Accessible for Reports, While Decision Portals Block Scrapers

Indonesian judicial data is split between open annual PDF publications and web-application-firewall (WAF) protected decision repositories. Compliance requires treating public accessibility as distinct from redistribution permission under national privacy and court-disclosure rules.

Gap Analysis Shows Strong Macro Metrics but Missing Decision-Level Schemas

While court-level aggregates and regulatory inventories are mature, decision-level Peninjauan Kembali (PK) annotation lacks a unified schema, automated PII verifier, and human-reviewed pilot corpus.

Six Operational Risks Require Deterministic Gates Before Publication

Open justice research fails if it leaks personal data, misstates judicial reasoning, or breaches source-access rules. Every risk below is paired with a fail-closed control modeled on the JNPDI governance gate.

Risk Likelihood Impact Deterministic Mitigation
WAF blocking on putusan3 HIGH Medium Halt automated crawlers on HTTP/2 403; collect 50–100 PK PDFs via manual browser export or formal PPID request.
PII exposure in decision PDFs MEDIUM Critical Run automated regex/NER screening plus mandatory human privacy review against UU PDP and SK KMA 2-144/2022.
LLM citation or reasoning hallucination HIGH Critical Enforce exact-span quote extraction, statutory hierarchy checks, and human legal verification on 100% of pilot records.
Premature public release Low High Keep all Kaggle datasets Private and stage code on feature branches until explicit human sign-off.
Silent missing-data imputation Medium High Enforce strict zero-imputation CI checks (from Scorecard/IOJO) with explicit completeness flags.
Mechanical Estonian transplant Medium Medium Benchmark Estonian e-File principles (interoperability, provenance) while adapting to Indonesian procedural law.

Estonia-Inspired Architecture Connects Local Verification to Open Repositories

Applying Estonia's principle of secure interoperability without unnecessary centralisation, the architecture links existing Master Court Registry identifiers to a four-stage open pipeline running entirely on Rp0 infrastructure.

First Pilot Plan Targets 50–100 PK Decisions Across Stages 1–6

To avoid duplicating IOJO or Scorecard, the pilot will operate as a dedicated PK Decision Annotation module linking into indonesia-open-justice-observatory and indonesia-master-court-registry-2026.

Stages 1–2: Source & Privacy Gate

Assemble 50–100 manually exported, QR-verifiable PK decisions spanning civil, criminal, religious, and administrative chambers. Execute SK KMA 2-144/2022 and UU PDP screening checklist.

Stages 3–4: Schema & Pilot Extraction

Finalize 9-part JSON/CSV annotation schema (case ID, legal issues, arguments, statutory provisions, reasoning, holdings, disposition, precedents, separated commentary). Run local extraction and human verification.

Stages 5–6: QA & Gated Staging

Run independent QA (100% provenance check, zero-imputation test, citation verification). Stage private Kaggle package and GitHub PR; pause for explicit human approval before any public release.

Do not equate downloads or website traffic with public trust; trust rests on verifiable provenance, strict privacy controls, and zero-hallucination legal provenance.
