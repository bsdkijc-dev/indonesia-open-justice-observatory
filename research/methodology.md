# Methodology — Indonesia Open Justice Observatory (IOJO)

## 1. Six Analytical Dimensions
1. **Transparency:** Availability, completeness, timeliness, downloadability, metadata, and source traceability.
2. **Accessibility:** Availability by court/region, ease of locating decisions, plain-language explanations, digital accessibility limits.
3. **Efficiency:** Case volume, disposition, clearance rate (where valid), pending caseload, processing duration (where dates exist).
4. **Accountability:** Verifiable indicators, reproducibility, source-to-indicator consistency, correction mechanisms.
5. **Quality of Information:** Metadata completeness, duplicate records, missingness, source reliability, validation status, provenance.
6. **Public Value:** Utility for researchers, citizens, legal practitioners, and policymakers.

## 2. Analytical Approach
- **Descriptive First:** Establish verified baselines, distributions, and missingness patterns before any inferential modeling.
- **No Unfounded Causality:** Do not claim causal effects without an explicit quasi-experimental or longitudinal identification strategy.
- **Stratified Court Analysis:** Stratify by `court_type`, `jurisdiction`, `province`, and reporting period.
