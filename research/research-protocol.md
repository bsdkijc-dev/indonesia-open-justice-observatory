# Research Protocol — Indonesia Open Justice Observatory (IOJO)

**Project Period:** 2027–2030  
**Research Director:** Cecep Mustafa, Ph.D.

## Research Questions (RQ01–RQ12)

| ID | Research Question | Primary Dimension(s) |
|---|---|---|
| **RQ01** | How much judicial performance information is publicly accessible? | Transparency, Accessibility |
| **RQ02** | How complete and consistent is published judicial information? | Transparency, Quality of Information |
| **RQ03** | How do case volumes vary across courts, jurisdictions, and years? | Efficiency |
| **RQ04** | What can available data tell us about case resolution and case clearance? | Efficiency, Accountability |
| **RQ05** | What information is available about case processing duration? | Efficiency, Transparency |
| **RQ06** | How accessible are published judgments? | Accessibility |
| **RQ07** | How complete and timely is judgment publication? | Transparency, Quality of Information |
| **RQ08** | What patterns exist in appeal and review processes? | Efficiency, Accountability |
| **RQ09** | What geographical inequalities can be observed in access to judicial information and services? | Accessibility, Public Value |
| **RQ10** | Which important judicial performance questions cannot currently be answered using public data? | Quality of Information, Accountability |
| **RQ11** | How can public information be made easier for ordinary citizens to understand? | Public Value, Accessibility |
| **RQ12** | Which improvements could be evaluated through future judicial policy research? | Public Value, Accountability |
